    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ's)

    How does HealthBuddy’s Risk Predictor work?

    Enter your symptoms and lifestyle info; HealthBuddy analyzes patterns and returns risk signals with explanations.

    What data does the system require?

    Provide concise symptom descriptions and basic lifestyle info (e.g., age, sleep, activity) for better insights.

    What inputs does HealthBuddy support?

    Text inputs are supported; image uploads are not required for HealthBuddy.

    How long does a prediction take?

    Results appear in seconds, depending on server load.

    How accurate are HealthBuddy predictions?

    Models are validated on held-out datasets; individual results vary by input quality.

    What does HealthBuddy confidence score mean?

    Confidence reflects model certainty; higher scores indicate stronger evidence.

    How often is HealthBuddy updated?

    We update models regularly with new validated data.

    What are HealthBuddy error rates?

    Validation metrics are reviewed routinely and improved over time.

    How can I help improve HealthBuddy?

    You can submit feedback in the app; it helps refine the models.

    How is my data protected?

    All inputs are encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest; we follow HIPAA-aligned practices.

    Does HealthBuddy store my data?

    We retain data only as long as necessary for audit and improvement; you may request deletion at any time.

    Who can access my HealthBuddy data?

    Only you (and any providers you authorize) can view your results—HealthBuddy staff do not access personal data.

    Is HealthBuddy data anonymized?

    Personal identifiers are stripped before model training; only de-identified data is used for research.

    Can I delete my HealthBuddy data?

    Yes—visit account settings and click “Delete my data.” We’ll remove all data within 30 days.

    Do I need an account to use HealthBuddy?

    Yes—creating an account lets you save scan history, receive notifications, and manage your data securely.

    Where can I view past reports?

    Open the report download links shown after a run; saved reports are accessible via the Reports section.

    Can I export my HealthBuddy results?

    Yes—you can download a PDF report for each scan from the “Result Details” page.

    How do I interpret HealthBuddy risk levels?

    Risk levels are categorized as Low, Moderate, or High based on probability thresholds defined by clinical guidelines.

    Where can I get help or support?

    Visit the “Contact Us” page for support from our HealthBuddy team.
